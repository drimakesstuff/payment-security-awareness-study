About the Research

This research explores how students use digital payments and how carefully they protect themselves while doing so. Using survey data collected from students, the study analyzes payment habits, security practices, fraud awareness, and exposure to scam attempts. The findings are presented through interactive visualizations, with a personal risk assessment that helps users understand how their own payment and security habits compare.
